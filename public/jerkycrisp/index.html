The Problem

Stop Fueling
with Syrup.

When you are deep in the pain cave or facing a rugged, off-grid scenario, your body rejects sweet, artificial energy gels. You crave salt. You need sustained fat. You need real food that doesn't freeze into a rock in the snow or melt into a mess in the Texas heat.

Most commercial survival bars are just glorified candy. We built the exact opposite.

Commercial Bars

High-glycemic sugars deliver a spike then a crash — exactly when you need sustained output. Artificial sweeteners cause GI distress under exertion stress.

Extreme Temperature Failure

Standard energy bars freeze solid below 20°F and turn to oily mush above 95°F. Neither works when you're on a winter rucksack or a summer desert crossing.

Sodium Deficit

You lose 500–1500mg of sodium per hour of intense exertion. Most commercial bars replace almost none of it. Cramping, performance decline, and hyponatremia follow.
